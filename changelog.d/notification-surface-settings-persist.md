### Fixed

- **Notification look settings are kept across a restart.** The pane border
  ring, its flash, the taskbar flash and the pane glow opacity in Settings ›
  Notifications are now saved with the rest of the session and restored on the
  next start, including a switched-off toggle. Before, every restart (and every
  update) turned them back on and reset the opacity to 60%. Older profiles keep
  the defaults until you change them.
